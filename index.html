The message was encrypted. The screenshot was shared.

Quick reminders & saving your work

Plaintext: a readable original message. Ciphertext: its encrypted form. Key: a value used by the encryption method. In our Caesar example, the key is the shift.

Encryption helps protect message content. A recipient who can read the message can still copy it. A Caesar cipher has very few possible shifts, so it is unsuitable for real private data.
